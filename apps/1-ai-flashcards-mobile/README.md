# AI Flashcards — mobile

**Complexity score:** 10

## Purpose

The mobile companion for the AI Flashcards project: an Expo/React Native app where learners review AI-generated flashcards on the go.

## Complexity Breakdown

- **Domain complexity:** Low — simple flashcard review and spaced-repetition basics.
- **Architecture complexity:** Low — single-screen Expo app, no shared workspace deps yet.
- **Infrastructure complexity:** Low — Expo managed workflow, no custom native modules.

## AI Usage

AI assists with generating flashcard content and with spec/test generation as screens are added.
